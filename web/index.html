A top-down 1v1 hockey sim. Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the game it is modelled on; uses none of its assets.

Controls

    Dash (Shift), lift (E) and slow walk (Space) are UNVERIFIED guesses at the real game's bindings. The slow walk's speeds are placeholders (the real game's braking is unmeasured). Left mouse (raise the stick while held) is also unverified; its height range is a placeholder.

    You are BLUE, attacking the top net. 5:00 clock, a 5-goal lead ends the match (mercy), a tie at 0:00 goes to next-goal overtime.
